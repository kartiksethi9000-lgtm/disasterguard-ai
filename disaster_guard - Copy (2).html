What's real vs. simulated in this demo: The map is real (OpenStreetMap tiles, real city coordinates). Weather readings are a local simulation — realistic per-city climate profiles (Mumbai/Kolkata/Guwahati skew flood-prone, Bhubaneswar/Chennai skew cyclone, Indore skews heat) that drift over time and occasionally spike, rather than a live external feed, since this sandboxed demo can't reliably make outbound API calls. Risk scores are computed with the transparent rule-based thresholds shown in the Risk Engine panel — a genuine trained ML predictor would need historical disaster-outcome data (e.g. NDMA/NOAA incident records) to train against. SMS dispatch is simulated in the log: real delivery needs a small backend holding your Twilio Account SID/Auth Token, since those secrets can't live in browser code.
